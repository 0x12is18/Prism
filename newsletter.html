This Week's Big Words
Germinate
When a seed sprouts and begins to grow roots and a shoot.
Predict
Making a smart guess about what will happen next, using what you already know.
Pattern
Something that repeats in a regular, predictable order — like ABAB!
Symmetry
When both sides of a shape or picture look exactly the same — like our butterflies!
